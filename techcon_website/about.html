History

TechCon began in 2015 as a small gathering of 50 developers in a local university. Over the past decade, it has grown into one of the largest tech conferences in Africa, attracting over 5,000 attendees annually from 30+ countries.

From humble beginnings to global recognition, TechCon continues to evolve, adapting to new technologies and fostering innovation in the African tech ecosystem.

Mission

Our mission is to empower the next generation of African technologists by providing world-class learning, networking, and collaboration opportunities.

We believe in inclusive innovation — bringing together developers, designers, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders to solve real-world problems.
